LabZero Mama

Women's health: a clinic day

    About this prototype

    LabZero Mama is an open-source simulation for training nurses, midwives, doctors and pharmacists to recognise and manage dangerous conditions in pregnancy, birth and the newborn period. It runs in a browser and works offline.

    The simulation opens on a busy public clinic on a Monday morning. Five women wait in the queue: severe pre-eclampsia at 34 weeks, endometriosis, cervical cancer in a woman living with HIV, intimate partner violence in early pregnancy, and severe depression in a 22-year-old medical student. Trainees follow each one from the file room and the nurse's vitals station to the clinic doctor and, where needed, the hospital.

    Safety by design. All patients are fictional. The patient can only say lines from her approved case script. The dialogue layer only chooses which approved line answers a question; scoring rules, not AI, decide whether an action was safe. Learner records stay on this device.

    Tuto, your tutor

    Choose a mode in the lobby. Learn with Tuto gives a briefing at every step, hints on request (a nudge, a bigger hint, then the answer) and a review after each step. Practice keeps the hints, but each costs 5 points. Assessment switches Tuto off. Tuto teaches only from the approved case content: the same reasons and explanations the scoring uses.

    Patient dialogue

    The language model receives only the trainee's question and the list of approved question topics. It never sees learner records and never writes the patient's words.
